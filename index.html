Homebound documentation reference

Connect the condition to the limitation

For Medicare, illness or injury must create a need for a device, another person, or special transportation to leave home, or make leaving medically inadvisable. The patient must also normally be unable to leave, and leaving must require considerable effort.

Include the supporting details

Document distance, device, assistance level, symptoms with activity, rest breaks, recovery time, steps, and transfer difficulty. A diagnosis or “taxing effort” alone is insufficient.

Common supporting limitations

Weakness, poor endurance, impaired balance, shortness of breath, limiting pain, activity restrictions, transfer difficulty, neurological impairment, confusion, severe vision impairment, and severe psychiatric symptoms may contribute when they cause the actual difficulty leaving.

Medical appointments, religious services, and some brief or infrequent outings can be consistent with homebound status. A walker, oxygen, age, a wound, or lack of transportation alone does not establish eligibility.
